Help Center

Find answers to common questions below. If you need more help, contact us.

📅 Booking & Reservations

How do I book a room?

Click "Book now" on any room card, fill in your details and dates, then confirm. Your booking is saved locally (demo).

Can I modify my booking?

Currently, please contact the host directly. We are building self‑service tools.

How far in advance can I book?

Up to 6 months in advance for most properties.

Do I get a confirmation?

Yes, a demo email is shown on screen. In production, real emails will be sent.

🏡 Hosting Your Property

How do I list my property?

Go to "Become a host" and fill out the form. Your room appears immediately.

How much does hosting cost?

Listing is free. Sawa Rooms takes 0% commission. You keep everything.

What photos should I upload?

Use clear, well‑lit photos of the room, bathroom, and common areas. High‑quality images attract more guests.

How are ratings calculated?

Guests rate stays after check‑out. The average rating shows on your listing.

💰 Payments & Cancellation

What payment methods are accepted?

We support Mobile Money (MTN/Airtel), Visa, and Mastercard. In demo mode, payments are simulated.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before check‑in. After that, first night is non‑refundable.

Can I pay in UGX?

Yes, all prices are shown in Ugandan Shillings. You can pay via Mobile Money or cash on arrival (host-dependent).

Is there a booking fee?

No. Sawa Rooms charges zero commission to both guests and hosts.

🛡️ Safety & Trust

Is Sawa Rooms safe?

We verify hosts and provide 24/7 customer support. Always communicate through our platform.

Can I trust the reviews?

Reviews are left only by guests who have completed a stay. Fake reviews are removed.

What if a host cancels?

We will help you find a similar or better stay at no extra cost.

How do I report a problem?

Use the "Report" button on the booking page or email safety@sawarooms.ug.

🔧 Technical & Account

I forgot my password

Click "Forgot password" on the sign‑in page. We'll send a reset link.

Is there a mobile app?

Not yet, but our website works perfectly on mobile browsers.

How do I filter rooms?

Use the filters on the "Stays" page: price range, district, rating, property type, and amenities.

Why do I see a skeleton loader?

It’s a loading animation while room data is prepared – a modern user experience detail.

🌍 General Questions

Which cities are covered?

Kampala, Jinja, Mbarara, Entebbe, Gulu, Fort Portal, Arua, Mbale, Lira, Kabale – and more being added weekly.

How many rooms are on Sawa Rooms?

Currently over 70 unique listings across Uganda, with new rooms added daily by hosts.

Can I change the website theme?

Not yet, but we plan to add dark/light mode soon.

Do you have live chat?

Not yet. For urgent issues, email help@sawarooms.ug and we reply within 2 hours.

How do you make money?

Currently we are a free platform. In future we may introduce optional premium features.

Are hotel rooms included?

Yes, we list guesthouses, hotels, hostels, and private homes.
